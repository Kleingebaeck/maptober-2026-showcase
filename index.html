MAPTOBER 2026 • OFFICIAL SCORING SYSTEM

How Jury Votes, Size Bonuses, and Homeruns are calculated

1. Score Formula per Mapart

Each mapart is rated from 1 to 10 points by the Maptober Jury. The average jury rating is combined with a fixed Size Bonus:

Total Map Score = Ø Jury Rating (1–10 pts) + Size Bonus (+1 to +10 pts)
2. Size Bonus Brackets
Tier Area (Maps) Examples Bonus
Mini / Standard 1 Map 1×1 (incl. 3D) +1 pt
Small 2 – 4 Maps 1×2, 2×1, 3×1, 2×2 +3 pts
Medium 5 – 9 Maps 2×3, 3×2, 5×1, 4×2, 3×3 +5 pts
Large 10 – 20 Maps 3×4, 5×3, 6×3, 4×5 +8 pts
Mega Mapart 21+ Maps 7×3, 5×5, 8×8, 9×6, 16×10 +10 pts
3. Homerun Bonus (All 31 Days) +35 POINTS

Every artist or team that submitted a valid mapart for all 31 days of October earns a +35 point Homerun Bonus. Congratulations to CherryTeam, SteinerndeMedusa, Endenjoyers, and KINGS OF CLOUD for completing all 31 days!
